# Novelty firewall

The manuscript must not claim that it is the first to model SOC recovery, storage flexibility, dynamic operating envelopes, or service duration. It must explicitly distinguish:

| Prior line | What it provides | What remains absent here |
|---|---|---|
| Evans et al. (2022) | exact aggregate discharge/loss/recovery capacity for heterogeneous storage | feeder AC voltage, thermal, phase, PV/load, and spatial placement coupling |
| Applied Energy security-flexibility work (e.g., Xiao et al., 2025) | one-event security-duration flexibility | recovery deadline and repeated-event deliverability |
| multi-period flexibility/DOE work | time coupling or network envelopes | a jointly auditable delivery--recovery contract under unbalanced AC physics |

No formal guarantee is claimed for the full nonlinear AC system. The proposed envelope is an inner candidate set generated by a reduced-order model and then audited with full OpenDSS AC power flow across held-out trajectories. Claims use “AC-audited” or “validated under sampled scenarios,” not universal guarantee.


## Additional nearby work

The paper also distinguishes the service--recovery viability composition from one-service rebound formulations, multi-activation demand-response scheduling, and duration/dependability matrices. The claimed object is the sequence-level BESS contract with repeated re-arm events and explicit terminal SOC deadlines, followed by nonlinear AC replay; these components are evaluated together rather than presented as a new generic dynamic feasible-region aggregation.
